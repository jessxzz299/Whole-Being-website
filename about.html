ABOUT WHOLE BEING

What is
Whole Being?

Whole Being is an invitation to slow down, reconnect with yourself and create space to understand what is happening within you.

It offers a gentle way of exploring your thoughts, feelings, experiences and patterns, using the natural elements as a guide.

Rather than following a fixed process, each experience is shaped around you and what you need in that moment.

It is a space for curiosity, reflection and self-discovery — allowing you to pause, listen inwardly and explore yourself as a whole.

What Is The Elemental Framework?

How the sessions work

All sessions include an exploration of the elements and a sound healing meditation.

At the beginning of our session, we'll take some time to talk about what you would like to explore. Together, we'll develop an intention and a loose plan for our time.

From there, I'm happy to guide the session intuitively, or to be led by your questions, reflections and feedback.

Most naturally, a dynamic between the two occurs: I lead, you respond, and we shape the session together.

There is no rigid formula for what happens next.

The work is open, responsive and fluid — just like Whole Being.

You don't need to arrive knowing exactly what you want to explore.

You can bring a question, a feeling, a situation, a pattern, a desire — or simply a sense that something is asking for your attention.

We begin where you are and discover where the session wants to go from there.
